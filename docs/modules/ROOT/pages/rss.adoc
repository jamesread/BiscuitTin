= RSS headlines

When the home screen is idle, configured RSS and Atom feeds show as rotating article summaries: the feed name, how recent the piece is, the title, and a short plain-text excerpt, in a single rounded card at the bottom left.

image::screenshots/home-idle-rss.png[RSS headlines on the idle home screen]
Articles slide in from the right and out to the left.
Each one stays at least a minute (the same steps as the background interval, from *More > Settings*).
An article is not shown again in the same session.

The tiles, menus and hints still fade out as usual (see xref:controls.adoc#_idle_and_hiding_the_tiles[idle]).
Headlines fade in with that transition.
They are gone again as soon as any input wakes the tiles.

== Configuration

Feeds are off until you add them. Each entry is an `http` or `https` URL, optionally with a display name that overrides the feed's own title.

[source,yaml]
----
rss:
  interval: 900          # refresh feeds, seconds (60 to 86400)
  rotateSeconds: 60      # how long each article stays on screen (60 to 86400)
  feeds:
    - https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/rss.xml
    - name: The Guardian
      url: https://www.theguardian.com/uk/rss
----

A feed may be a URL string or `{url, name}`.
RSS 2.0 and Atom are both accepted.
HTML in titles and descriptions is stripped, and HTML entities and Windows-1252 punctuation are decoded.

Fetched XML is cached under `~/.cache/biscuittin/rss/` and reused when a refresh fails, so headlines keep working offline.

*More > Diagnostics* shows how many feeds and articles are loaded.

Leave `feeds` empty (or omit `rss`) to keep the idle screen as the photo only.
